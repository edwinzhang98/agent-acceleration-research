## 2. D-ledger additions

- **D301** — Page 3 goal notation. Deck 067daa4 wrote `min_{m: R_m(p) ≥ R_0} (T_success, v)  in the Pareto sense`. No source writes "in the Pareto sense" after a formula (Boyd & Vandenberghe 2004 whole-book scan; Ehrgott 2005; Miettinen 1999; Deb 2001; G1–G4). **Resolution:** `min_m (T_success(m,p), v(m,p))^⊤  s.t.  R_m(p) ≥ R_0`, with the Pareto meaning in the gloss ("no other design matches on both and beats on one"), after Sener & Koltun (NeurIPS 2018) Eq. 2 and Lin et al. (NeurIPS 2019) Eq. 1; the ^⊤ marks a vector so the min is not read as the scalar min{a, b} that Cost-of-Pass Eq. 5 uses. Fully formal form for the paper (calc., our transcription of Boyd p. 186): minimize (w.r.t. R²₊) (T_success, v) over m subject to R_m(p) ≥ R_0.
- **D302** — Source for the meaning of the vector min. The deck cited Kapoor et al. 2025, which writes no formula (G5). **Resolution:** cite Boyd & Vandenberghe (2004) §4.7 (4.56, 4.59, §4.7.5 pp. 181–183); new Part 1 reference entry. Kapoor stays as the agent-evaluation precedent (A0 3/5, verified abstract quote).
- **D303** — Cost-of-Pass and "Pareto" / App. C.8. Cost-of-Pass never says Pareto (0 hits, v1 and v2) and its frontier is a scalar min; App. C.8's remedy (i) is one of four and concerns impractical baselines (cheap random guessers). A0 3/5 now says the floor R_0 "is our choice, after App. C.8's remedy (i)" and "Cost-of-Pass never says Pareto".
- **D304** — Notation caution, no change: 𝓜 means the model-call interval set on page 4 and A0 (AsyncFC, adapted) and the model set in Cost-of-Pass's own Eq. 3, quoted verbatim in A0 3/5's source column. The two meet only in A0 3/5, where the Cost-of-Pass formula sits in the "source's own notation" column; page 3 uses no set symbol.
