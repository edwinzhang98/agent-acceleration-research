# 2026-09-29 — Deck audit: every cited work and every cited passage, checked at the source

Request (Edwin, 29 Sep): check every page for citations that do not exist or are wrong, citation-tier mistakes (top venue → first-party company source → strong-lab arXiv), arXiv works since published, and numbers or readings copied wrongly — a real audit, not a surface pass.

Method: the rendered deck (slides/agent-acceleration.html, 74 pages; the reference-list pages excluded as pages, their entries checked per work) was cut into 444 cited passages (table rows, cards, bullets, callouts, captions, definition rows) and grouped by work: 127 works (154 citation keys across the two parts) in 25 batches, plus the 53 works that Appendix B9 names in 3 batches. A 56-agent workflow: per batch one auditor opened each primary source (arXiv abs and versions, proceedings / OpenReview / DOI, vendor pages, code), checked the reference entry and venue status and every number, condition and reading in every passage; an independent checker per batch re-opened the sources for every flagged item (default: refuted) and spot-checked at least three passages the auditor had passed. Research files were used as pointers only. Workflow run wf_efaafe9e-c7f; raw results in the session scratchpad (audit-final.json).

Totals: 444 passages audited (auditor: 268 ok, 130 minor, 25 misreading, 12 wrong condition, 3 wrong number, 1 wrong attribution, 5 unverifiable); 180 works/entries checked. After the independent check: **146 claim errors confirmed or corrected** (high 8, medium 74, low 64); **33 reference-entry errors** (medium 7, low 26); 55 auditor reports refuted; 7 unverifiable; 3 further errors found by spot checks of passages the auditors had passed. Found while building the inventory: BrowserGym (Part 2 reference list) is no longer cited on any page since Part 2 page 8's callout was rewritten on 29 Sep.

## 2. D-ledger additions

- **D305 — TraceLab 3.8× and 5.3× (corrects D65, the D16 resolution and E19).** D65 and D16 say "prefill amplification 5.3×" appears in no version of the paper, that 3.8× is in §4.3 and that the word "amplification" is not used. All three are false for v1 and v2: "prefill amplification" 5.3× (append ÷ fresh, all steps) is in §7.3 (p. 13) and Table 12 (p. 14); user-initiated steps, where the cache misses fall, prefill 35.4× their new input (Table 12); the 3.8× sentence is in §1 (Introduction, p. 2) and is trace-wide, not per miss. research/2026-09-28-problem-definition.md l. 314 ("TraceLab v2 §7.3 reports 5.3×") was right. Deck: a3-1 wording (AU list).
- **D306 — Copilot "intra-turn concurrency 1.15" (E20).** 1.15 is the median LLM-call concurrency among the 63.3% of turns whose calls overlap at all; 36.7% of turns are strictly sequential; P90 1.4 (Liu et al., 2026, arXiv 2608.00101v1). It is not the concurrency of every turn. The 13.7% model / 2.0% tools / 80.1% idle figures are separate medians over multi-turn sessions of total session wall-clock (idle included), not a split of agent time. Deck: s05, s09, a1-3, a2-1.
- **D307 — OSWorld-Human "later steps up to 3× longer" (E23; dossier §5 item 5).** The 3× is Agent S2's (GPT-4.1, Fig. 3; first in v1, which studied S2 only); GTA1 (o3) rises ≈1.3–1.5× (Fig. 4, read off). The harnesses cap the screenshot history, so prompts grow mostly by text (GTA1 ≈3k → ≈17k tokens over 100 steps), not by one screenshot per earlier step; "dominated by the prefill stage" is the authors' inference from prompt size — no prefill/decode split or TTFT was measured. Deck: s07, s08, a1-2.
- **D308 — Waiting for the user, 80–92% (TraceLab vs Copilot).** The range joins two statistics: Copilot's 80.1% is the median per-session share over multi-turn sessions; TraceLab's 92.3% is pooled over all 4,265 sessions, dominated by sessions left open for hours or days (median session 0 s; 64.3% with gaps capped at 1 h). Deck: s09, a1-3.
- **D309 — KVFlow 1.83× (corrects the B9 correction row).** The camera-ready's Fig. 4b reproduces 1.83× on one H100 (Qwen2.5-32B, 8192-token prefix, two branches; calc. from bar heights: KVFlow 1.52× of plain SGLang, HiCache 0.83×), so it is not "absent from the camera-ready body" nor an A10G-only figure; 1.24× is one setting (4096/32/32), 1.29× the average over HiCache.
