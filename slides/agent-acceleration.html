1 · The problem1

What we mean by slow and expensive

  • One task is measured by four things: wall-clock, dollars per attempt, model calls, per-field accuracy.
  • "Faster" counts only if per-field accuracy does not fall.
model calls≠ steps ≠ turns
inference time≠ wall-clock
cost per attempt≠ cost per success
estimate≠ stopwatch
AKapoor et al., TMLR 2025 (cost-controlled evaluation) Measurement rules: dossier §0.3

先定义再举证。四个量：每任务 wall-clock、每次尝试的美元、模型调用次数、字段级准确率。

四条"不等于"是后面每个数字的阅读规则：调用不等于步数不等于 turn；inference 时间不等于 wall-clock；每次尝试不等于每次成功；估计不等于掐表。

来源：Kapoor et al. "AI Agents That Matter"（TMLR 2025）提出成本受控的 agent 评测；规则本身汇总在 dossier §0.3。

Hundreds of model calls per task

来源等级 D：预印本，但出自 OSWorld 原团队（原 OSWorld 发表于 NeurIPS 2024；OpenAI、Anthropic 发布 computer-use 模型时都引用这个 benchmark）。作者名单待 references pass 补齐。

1 · The problem3

Where the time goes

87–97%
of wall-clock is the model: planning, judging, reflecting. Screenshots and actions: under 3.5%.
  • Later steps run up to 3× slower: every call re-sends the screenshot history.
  • Contrary measurement: one 2026 study finds the desktop sandbox above 70% of execution time.Neither study controlled caching. Screenshot-based agents only.
AAbhyankar et al., MLSys 2026 DAgentSysBench, arXiv 2608.15127 (Aug 2026)

E22：OSWorld-Human 把两个 agent 每一步拆开量，87–97% 在模型的 planning/judging/reflection；截图和执行动作不到 3.5%。E23：越靠后越慢，最多 3 倍，因为每步重发全部历史截图。

主动说出相反的测量：E97/D95，AgentSysBench 测到 sandbox 占 70% 以上；D101，两边都没控制 cache 状态。所以这页只能说"对截图型 agent、在这次测量里"。

来源等级：MLSys 2026 是 A；AgentSysBench 是预印本 D。

1 · The problem4

Accuracy is convex-expensive

~6×
output tokens for 13.0% → 20.6% completion: $25.5 → $72.4 per task
9×
cost for a 2-point difference: $171 vs $1,577 per 300 tasks
9.6×
tokens for +4.4 points, same model, more samples
  • Mechanism: cost accumulates quadratically over steps, because each step re-reads the history.
DOSWorld 2.0, arXiv (Jul 2026) · HKU DHAL, arXiv 2510.11977 (Oct 2025) · Princeton DAgentic Test-Time Scaling for WebAgents, arXiv 2602.12276 (Feb 2026) AAbhyankar et al., MLSys 2026 (mechanism)

三个独立来源、同一个形状。E41：OSWorld 2.0，13.0→20.6% 要约 6 倍 output token，论文原话"每多一个点约 25–30K tokens"。E43：HAL，差 2 个点成本差 9 倍，贵到 Opus 4.1 没跑（约 $20,000 一次）。E44：唯一固定模型的实验，9.6 倍 token 换 4.4 个点。

注意：三个 headline 数字全是预印本（D）；机制上的支撑是 MLSys 那篇的"成本随步数二次增长"（E45）。前两个是跨模型比较。如果老板不接受预印本，这页降级为补充证据。

1 · The problem5

Why now: agents are the majority workload

  • Agentic tokens overtook human tokens on OpenRouter around 1 Feb 2026; an agent request uses ~15× the tokens of a human request.
  • Gartner: agentic models need 5–30× the tokens per task of a chatbot; cost per agentic workflow to rise more than 5× by 2028.
  • Practitioners: latency is the #2 production barrier (20% of 1,340), yet only 14.8% of deployed teams call it critical.It shapes which tasks get deployed and how, not whether.
BOpenRouter, Jun 2026 CGartner, Mar & Aug 2026 CLangChain, 2026 AMeasuring Agents in Production, ICML 2026

E57：OpenRouter 自家 telemetry（450T+ tokens，2026 上半年），agent 流量约 2 月 1 日超过人类；每请求约 15 倍 token。只是一个平台，不是全行业。

E62/E55：Gartner 是分析师估计，没有公开样本。E68：LangChain 1,340 人调查，自选样本。E69/D80：MAP（ICML 2026 Oral）已部署团队里 14.8% 认为 latency 关键，15/20 系统"可以异步运行"——所以结论是 latency 决定部署方式，不是阻止部署。

不要用 88%、14×、70% cached 这几个数（D19、D70、D71，来源不足）。

1 · The problem6

Why now: speed is already for sale

  • Anthropic and OpenAI sell "fast mode": 2× the price for up to 2.5× the output speed.
  • OpenAI "Ultrafast": up to 14× output speed (preview, unpriced).
  • Anthropic moved Claude in Chrome's default to the smaller Haiku 4.5 "for faster, more responsive browsing".
  • Meanwhile the price of a token keeps falling: GPT-6 Luna lists at $0.10 / $0.50 per million.Cheaper tokens have not made tasks cheap.
BAnthropic pricing & release notes, 2026 BOpenAI pricing & API changelog, 2026

E76/E77：两家的 fast mode 都是 2 倍价格换最多 2.5 倍输出速度；E194：OpenAI 8 月的 Ultrafast 最高 14 倍（预览、未定价）；E80：Claude in Chrome 默认换成 Haiku 4.5。

§4.4：GPT-6 Luna 每百万 token $0.10/$0.50——token 在暴跌，但每任务成本没有跟着跌（E55）。这就引出下一页：成本单位不是 token。

来源全是 B：厂商价目表和发布说明是事实性一手资料，不是营销数字。

1 · The problem7

The problem in one sentence

An agent is a program, not a prompt: a chain of dependent model calls over a growing context, plus waits for tools. Cheaper tokens do not shorten the chain. Fewer, cheaper steps do.

  • Still unmeasured anywhere: a clean agent-vs-human wall-clock on the same tasks.Closest pair: a UI agent 1.69× slower than a person on simple Word tasks, 0.93× on harder ones (20 participants).
ALu et al., ACL 2025 (AXIS user study) Synthesis: dossier §2.3, §2.5

§2.3/§2.5 的合并结论：成本单位是"一步决策"，不是 token。这句话是整份 deck 的主线，后面"别人做了什么"和"我们做什么"都围绕"少走几步、每步更便宜"。

E161（AXIS 用户实验，ACL 2025）：目前唯一一对同任务、同参与者的 agent vs 人计时。follow-up 4 查了 17 个 benchmark，没有更干净的。所以不写"agent 比人慢 N 倍"，写"没人认真测过"，这本身是后面要做的事之一。
