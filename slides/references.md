# References for the deck and the Chinese briefs

Citation scheme (agreed 2026-09-27): in text, author–year — `(Abhyankar et al., 2026)`; organisation authors by name — `(XLANG Lab, 2026)`, `(Anthropic, 2026a)`. A dossier ledger ID in square brackets after the citation — `[E22]` — points to the entry in `Agent-Acceleration-Consolidated-Dossier-v3-2026-09-27.md` that holds the exact figure, measurement definition and URL; it is not a citation. On slides: the author–year tag on the slide, the full entry on the module's References slide (clickable or appendix if space is short).

Source types (replace the earlier A–E letters, same meaning):

| Type | Was | Cited? |
|---|---|---|
| conference or journal paper (published version cited when a preprint was accepted) | A | yes |
| vendor primary material: engineering docs, pricing pages, changelogs, router telemetry (vendor-stated multipliers labelled as such) | B | yes |
| industry report (analyst press release with stated method) | C | yes |
| preprint (arXiv), authors' institutions shown | D | yes, flagged |
| vendor marketing | E | no |
| source code; third-party measurement; our own calculation (calc.) | — | yes, labelled |

Authors, institutions, versions and dates below were checked against the arXiv HTML/abs pages and the ACL Anthology on 2026-09-27 unless marked "to confirm".

Source-credibility rule (Edwin, 2026-09-27, applied to both Chinese docs and to the deck): a preprint is used as evidence only if (i) its authors and institutions are verifiable on the arXiv page, (ii) the institution is a university, national lab or a company with a research group, (iii) its numbers come from a public benchmark with the task count and model stated, and (iv) it is not a single-author industry report. A preprint that fails any of these is treated as if the source did not exist: not in the evidence tables, not on slides, not in the reference list; it is listed once under "Not used" with the reason. Qualitative-only mentions must also pass the author/institution check. A venue counts only when the official venue page or the arXiv page says so; a template copyright line ("AAAI 2027") or "under review" does not. Third-party summaries (Gemini/ChatGPT runs) never supply an author name without an arXiv check — the MFS/GEPA misattribution (D146) came from one.

Ledger (2026-09-28): `references-ledger.md` maps every source of dossier v3 to one reference, with the same keys in `references.bib`; its class D is not filtered by the rule above. This list was reconciled with it on 2026-09-28: where a work was published, venue, year, title and author order follow the published version as shown on the venue page (ICML/ICLR/NeurIPS pages, the TMLR BibTeX on jmlr.org/tmlr, Crossref DOI metadata); otherwise they follow the arXiv abs page. Checks, and D152–D154, are in `research/2026-09-28-references-merge-check.md`.
